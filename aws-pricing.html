Key Pricing Facts for the Exam
EC2
Pay per second (Linux) or per hour (Windows). Spot saves up to 90% but can be interrupted. Reserved saves up to 72% for 1–3 year commits.
S3
Pay for storage + requests + data out. Inbound always free. First 100 GB/mo outbound free. Deep Archive: $0.00099/GB — cheapest AWS storage.
Lambda
1M requests free/month — always. Pay per 1M requests + GB-second of compute. 400,000 GB-sec free/month. Zero cost when idle.
RDS
Multi-AZ doubles instance cost but provides automatic failover. Storage billed separately per GB/month. Reserved RDS saves up to 69%.
Data Transfer
Inbound is always free. First 100 GB/mo outbound free. Same-region and to CloudFront free. Cross-region and to internet charged.
Free Tier
Always Free: Lambda 1M req, DynamoDB 25 GB. 12-Month Free: 750 hr EC2 t2.micro, 5 GB S3, 25 GB RDS, 15 GB transfer.
